Questions about insurance?

Coverage for cranial prostheses varies by plan and individual circumstances. We encourage clients to contact their insurer to ask about eligibility, documentation, reimbursement, and any required prescription. If assistance is needed, you can schedule a consultation and we can discuss next steps.

Ask your insurer

Confirm whether your plan covers a cranial prosthesis and what terminology or benefit category it uses.

Check documentation

Ask whether a prescription, letter of medical necessity, invoice, or specific billing code is required.

Discuss next steps

Email or schedule a consultation with Rx Wig Precision to discuss the information you have and what documentation is required.
